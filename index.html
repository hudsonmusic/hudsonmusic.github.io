About Me

Hi, I'm Hudson Collins, a freshman at Florida State University majoring in Marketing. I'm from West Palm Beach, Florida.

Outside of class I produce music, and I also work in nightlife, so I spend a lot of time around events, promotion, and figuring out what gets people to show up. That's a big part of why I chose marketing.

This portfolio collects my revised project, the artifacts that show how my ideas developed, and a reflection on how I've grown as a writer over the course of ENC 2135.
